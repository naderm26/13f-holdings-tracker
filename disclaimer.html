Data Is Stale By Design

13F filings are required to be submitted within 45 days after the end of each calendar quarter. The holdings shown on this site therefore reflect positions as of a date that is at minimum 45 days in the past and potentially much older. Fund managers may have dramatically increased, reduced, or fully exited any position shown on this site since the most recent filing date. What you see here is not current — it is historical disclosure data.
